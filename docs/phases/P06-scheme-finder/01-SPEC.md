# Phase P06 — Specification: Scheme Finder & Eligibility Engine

## 1. Goals
- Deliver the Scheme Finder catalog page (`/schemes`) with category filtering (Education, Agriculture, Disability/Divyang, Women & Child, Social Welfare).
- Implement natural-language scheme query processing (e.g., "scholarships for OBC students in Maharashtra").
- Deliver scannable `SchemeCard` components displaying benefits, eligibility bullets, and verification badges.
- Deliver detailed scheme view (`/schemes/:id`) with explicit separation between Sahayak plain-language summary and official government source.

## 2. Requirements

### Functional Requirements
- **FR-P06-01 (Faceted Filtering Engine):** Filter `schemes.json` by category, target beneficiary group, and keyword.
- **FR-P06-02 (Natural Language Scheme Search):** Connects to `AssistantService` with `intent: "scheme_discovery"`; uses keyword matching and semantic filtering to retrieve matching records.
- **FR-P06-03 (Scannable SchemeCard Component):** Render scheme name, category badge, short plain-language explanation, key benefits teaser, and "View Details" button.
- **FR-P06-04 (Scheme Detail Page):** Render full scheme profile:
  - Scheme overview and plain-language summary.
  - Who can apply? (Detailed eligibility checklist).
  - Benefits & subsidies provided.
  - Mandatory documents required.
  - Official Authority & Source Attribution (`OfficialSource` component).
  - Direct "Apply on Official Portal" link with verified URL.
- **FR-P06-05 (Zero-Result Handling):** If no matching scheme is found, show an honest empty state with suggested queries and links to myScheme.

### Non-Functional Requirements
- **Trust & Verification:** Displays verification status (`verified`, `lastVerified: "2026-09-25"`).
- **Responsive Layout:** Responsive multi-column grid on desktop, single-column stack on mobile.

## 3. Inputs & Outputs
- **Inputs:** `Scheme` records from `schemes.json` and user search strings.
- **Outputs:** Interactive UI on `/schemes` and `/schemes/:id`.

## 4. Modules & Contracts
- `core/schemes/scheme-repository.ts`
- `core/schemes/scheme-service.ts`
- `components/schemes/SchemeCard.tsx`
- `components/schemes/SchemeDetails.tsx`
- `components/schemes/EligibilityList.tsx`
- `components/schemes/OfficialSourceBox.tsx`

## 5. Constraints & Out-of-Scope
- No synthetic schemes generated by AI.
- No direct form filling or application submission.
