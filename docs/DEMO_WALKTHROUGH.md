# Sahayak AI — Demo Walkthrough Script

**For hackathon judges, demo presenters, and evaluators**

> **Tip:** The entire demo runs offline using the built-in `DemoProvider` — no Wi-Fi required.
> Start the app with: `npm run dev`

## Quick Reference

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Start app | `npm run dev` (http://localhost:3000) |

## 3-Minute Pitch Script

### [0:00 – 0:30] — Open & Problem Statement

**Say:**
> "India has over 100 government welfare schemes and 60+ digitized services. But most citizens — especially in Maharashtra — can't navigate them: websites are in English, processes have 6-8 steps, and a wrong move means starting over. Sahayak AI solves this."

**Show:** Open http://localhost:3000

- Point to the badge: "महाराष्ट्र शासन नागरिक सहाय्य"
- Point to the search bar and quick-action chips

---

### [0:30 – 1:10] — Service Discovery (Journey 1)

**Say:**
> "A senior citizen in Nashik needs an income certificate. They don't know where to start. They just say this—"

**Do:**
1. Click the **"उत्पन्न प्रमाणपत्र"** chip  
   *(or type: "I need an income certificate")*
2. Wait 2–3 seconds for the response

**Show:**
- The Sahayak Guidance card with service explanation
- Steps preview (first 3 of 6)
- The Sahayak vs Official distinction
- Click **"मार्गदर्शन सुरू करा →"** (Start Guided Task)

**Say:**
> "Sahayak tells them exactly what they need — in Marathi. And it distinguishes between its guidance and the official government portal."

---

### [1:10 – 1:40] — Deterministic Task Journey (Journey 2)

**Show:** Service detail page `/services/income-certificate`

**Point to:**
- Step roadmap (6 numbered steps)
- Current step with description in Marathi
- Document checklist
- Payment safety notice on Step 5
- Official source link at the bottom

**Say:**
> "The steps are deterministic — not generated by AI each time. They're sourced from the official Aaple Sarkar portal. Sahayak never makes up government information."

---

### [1:40 – 2:05] — Scheme Finder (Journey 3)

**Do:**
1. Click **Schemes** in navigation (or `/schemes`)
2. Type "scholarship" in the search box
3. Click on the MahaDBT scholarship card

**Show:**
- Eligibility criteria
- Benefits list
- Required documents
- Official source badge linking to mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in

**Say:**
> "For welfare schemes, Sahayak shows verified data from the actual government portals. If a scheme isn't in our verified registry, we say so — we never fabricate eligibility."

---

### [2:05 – 2:25] — Explain Screen (Journey 4)

**Do:**
1. Navigate to `/explain-screen`
2. Click **"🏛️ Aaple Sarkar Login Form"** demo chip
3. Click **"Explain This Screen"**

**Show:**
- Structured breakdown of form elements
- Simple-language explanation
- Glossary of civic terms

**Say:**
> "For citizens who encounter unfamiliar government forms, they can screenshot the page and upload it. Sahayak explains every field in plain Marathi — without storing any image data."

---

### [2:25 – 2:45] — Language & Accessibility

**Do:**
1. Click **"मराठी"** button in header (if not already in Marathi)
2. Click **"हिंदी"** — show UI switches instantly
3. Click the **♿ accessibility** button
4. Enable **Large Text** slider
5. Enable **High Contrast** toggle

**Say:**
> "Sahayak supports English, Marathi, and Hindi — switching instantly. Accessibility is built-in: large text, high contrast, voice input, and read-aloud output."

---

### [2:45 – 3:00] — Trust & Safety Distinction

**Point to any response card showing:**
- 🤖 Orange "Sahayak Guidance" badge
- 🏛️ Blue "Official Government Information" card below it

**Say:**
> "Sahayak always separates its guidance from the official government source. We never impersonate a government department. Every response includes: 'Verify on the official portal before submitting.'"

**Optionally, show:** `/extension` page — install steps for Chrome side panel

---

## Known Limitations

1. **Voice**: Web Speech API requires Chrome and microphone permission. Falls back to text input gracefully.
2. **Vision AI**: Explain Screen uses DemoProvider responses offline. Live Gemini requires `AI_API_KEY`.
3. **Extension field highlighting**: Requires actual live portal DOM — works on real Aaple Sarkar pages in Chrome.
4. **Scheme data**: Currently 5 verified schemes in registry. Production expansion requires manual verification against official portals.
