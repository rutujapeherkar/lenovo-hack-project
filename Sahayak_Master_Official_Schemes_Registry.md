# Sahayak AI — Master Official Scheme & Portal Registry
## Maharashtra + National Government Schemes

**Research date:** 25 September 2026  
**Purpose:** Source-of-truth specification for the Sahayak AI scheme/service database.

---

## 0. Critical Scope Rule

Sahayak is **Maharashtra-first**, but users may also ask about Government of India schemes that are available to or relevant for Maharashtra residents.

There is an important distinction:

1. **Maharashtra Government schemes/services** must resolve to an official Maharashtra Government department/portal whenever the Maharashtra Government is the implementing authority.
2. **Central Government schemes available in Maharashtra** must resolve to the official Government of India application portal or the official Maharashtra implementation portal, depending on how the scheme is actually administered.
3. **National scholarship schemes** must resolve to the official National Scholarship Portal when NSP is the official application channel.
4. **Disability schemes** must resolve to the actual official portal used by that scheme: Maharashtra DEPwD/Divyang Sahayak, MahaDBT, NSP, UDID, PM-DAKSH, or another government portal as officially specified.
5. **Never generate an official URL with AI.** URLs must come from this verified registry or from a future verified government-source synchronization process.

### Important limitation

It is **not technically responsible to claim that a static Markdown file can permanently contain every Government of India scheme**. The official National Portal of India currently exposes thousands of government schemes, and myScheme is a national discovery platform that is continuously updated. Therefore, Sahayak must use the official national scheme indexes as the authoritative discovery layer and maintain explicit verified records for schemes that Sahayak actively supports.

The same principle applies to Maharashtra: individual departments publish their own scheme directories and these can change over time.

Therefore this document has two layers:

- **Verified current catalogue:** schemes explicitly identified from official government sources during this research.
- **Authoritative discovery registries:** official government indexes that Sahayak must use to expand/refresh the catalogue without inventing information.

# 14. Rules for Application Buttons

## Rule 1 — Exact URL only

Never display:

> Visit the official website.

Instead display:

> **Government of Maharashtra — Aaple Sarkar**  
> [View Official Details]  
> [Apply on Official Portal]

---

## Rule 2 — No AI-generated URLs

The AI may identify the scheme.

The AI may explain the scheme.

The AI may simplify eligibility.

The AI must **not invent the application URL**.

---

## Rule 3 — Separate information and application URLs

Some schemes have:

```text
Information:
Government department page

Application:
Separate official portal
```

Store both.

---

## Rule 4 — Information-only schemes

Some schemes are institutional programmes and do not have a citizen application form.

Examples include:

- SIPDA
- DDRS
- Some rehabilitation/infrastructure programmes

For these:

```text
[View Official Scheme Information]
```

Do not show:

```text
[Apply Now]
```

unless an official citizen application route exists.

---

## Rule 5 — Dynamic portals

For portals such as NSP and MahaDBT, the application period and routing can change.

Therefore:

```text
lastVerified
applicationStatus
academicYear
applicationOpen
applicationClose
```

should be stored separately from the permanent scheme record.

# 15. National-Level Scheme Strategy

The national catalogue is too large and dynamic to safely hard-code into a single static Markdown file.

The official India.gov.in currently reports thousands of government schemes, while myScheme is specifically designed as a national one-stop scheme discovery platform. citeturn0search8turn0search10

Therefore Sahayak should implement:

### Tier 1 — Explicit verified records

High-value schemes that Sahayak directly explains and guides.

Examples:

- PM-KISAN
- Disability scholarships
- UDID
- AICTE Saksham
- PM-USP CSSS
- PM YASASVI
- National Means Cum Merit Scholarship
- SC Top Class Education
- ST Higher Education Scholarship

### Tier 2 — Official portal discovery

For schemes not yet explicitly indexed:

```text
User asks
   ↓
myScheme / India.gov official discovery
   ↓
Find matching scheme
   ↓
Official scheme information
   ↓
Official application route
```

### Tier 3 — Never fabricate

If the official application destination cannot be verified:

```text
Official application destination:
Not verified

[View Official Scheme Information]
```

# 16. Source Priority

Use this priority order:

```text
1. Official application portal
2. Official Maharashtra Government department
3. Official Government of India department/ministry
4. Official Maharashtra scheme directory
5. Official NSP / MahaDBT / Aaple Sarkar / UDID
6. myScheme / India.gov official index
7. Government PDF / Government Resolution
8. Third-party sources — research only
```

Third-party websites must **never** replace the official application link shown to a citizen.

# 20. Do Not Confuse These

### Government information ≠ application portal

A department page may explain a scheme while another government portal handles the application.

### Scheme ≠ Government Resolution

A GR may modify or establish a scheme but is not necessarily the application destination.

### Central scheme ≠ Maharashtra scheme

A central scheme can be implemented in Maharashtra without becoming a Maharashtra Government scheme.

### Portal ≠ scheme

MahaDBT, NSP, Aaple Sarkar and UDID are platforms containing multiple services/schemes.

# 22. Final Rule for Antigravity

When implementing the Sahayak scheme system:

```text
DO:
✓ Use verified official government sources
✓ Store exact URLs
✓ Store informationUrl and applicationUrl separately
✓ Store verification date
✓ Store application status
✓ Support English/Marathi/Hindi
✓ Link directly to official portals
✓ Update dynamic portals
✓ Use official discovery registries for expansion

DO NOT:
✗ Guess URLs
✗ Generate government links with AI
✗ Use Google search result URLs
✗ Use Wikipedia as the official source
✗ Use news websites as application destinations
✗ Use private agents
✗ Show "Visit government website" without a destination
✗ Show Apply Now when no official application route exists
✗ Treat every national scheme as permanently static
```
